# Box-wing concept: cabin layout dimensions (first cut)

Laid out to the brief's Appendix 1 (3-class MR) rules. The box wing does not change the cabin; it moves the wing box and puts the rear wing on twin fins at the tail.

## Galleys, lavatories, crew
- Trolleys: BC 28 x 10 trays / 28 = 10; YC+/YC 272 x 3 / 28 = 29.1 -> 30. Drawn: 40 (10 BC + 30 YC).
- Lavatories: BC 28 / 20 -> 2; YC+/YC 272 / 40 -> 7. Drawn: 9 (40 x 42 in = 1680 in², min 1650).
- Galley depth 34 in, every galley opens onto a 60 in door cross-aisle (>= 36 in trolley space). No YC trolley passes through BC.
- Attendants: service 2 + 2 + 7 = 11; exits 4 Type A pairs x 2 = 8. So 11 (matches the brief's App. 2 crew).
